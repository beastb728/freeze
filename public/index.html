Freeze

Point a few phones at one moment. Tap once. Get a scene you can walk around.

  1. Start a session and show the QR code to your friends. Nothing to install.
  2. Stand in a line in seat order and aim the circle at the same thing.
  3. The host taps Freeze. Every phone keeps the frame from the same instant.
  4. Open the link and drag to orbit the moment.
